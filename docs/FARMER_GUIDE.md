# KrishiMitra — Farmer User Guide

This guide explains the current version and is written in simple English. Full regional-language localization has **not** yet been completed.

## First day

1. Open **My farm → My farm** and select **Edit profile**. Enter your farm's name and location, country, currency, land area and area unit. Do not enter bank account or government ID information.
2. Open **Explore crops**. Use filters or the search box. Open a crop details card, review its *general* information, then press **Plan this crop**.
3. Open **Daily farm tasks** and create today's activities. Mark them finished as you complete them.

## Weekly routine

- Record irrigation and field work under **Daily farm tasks**.
- Save actual laboratory soil reports under **Soil health**. Do not infer fertilizer quantities from basic pH or NPK fields.
- Add field photos and observations in **Crop health** when you see visible changes. Take these to a qualified agronomist; this site does not identify disease from images.
- Enter buyer offers in **Market prices & buyer quotes** so you can compare estimated proceeds after costs.
- Save expenses and earnings under **Finance**.

## Harvest season

- Use **Harvest & yield** to calculate scenarios based on a yield number *you provide*; this is not an AI forecast.
- Record actual harvested quantity.
- Add a storage batch, record the lot, location and status.
- Keep **Market hub** produce drafts if you want to organize the details. They are **not published**, and buyers cannot see them.

## Weather

Search any city or region in **Weather** while online. Weather forecasts are informational, not a substitute for official local severe weather alerts. If the weather service is unavailable, the site should display a clear error.

## Keep data safe

Go to **My farm → Data & backups** and download a JSON backup. Save it in a private and safe location. After changing phones or browsers, use **Restore a backup** on the new browser. Restoring replaces existing data on that browser. The platform does not have cloud sync in this version.

## If the app is offline

Once the site is opened and cached on HTTPS or localhost, core pages can be accessed offline. Records save locally. External weather requests, public portals and external links require internet access.
